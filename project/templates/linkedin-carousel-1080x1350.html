Three page types, one rhythm: dark cover → light content → dark CTA. The mode flip is what makes the deck feel like a deck rather than a slideshow of posts.

Length: 7–10 pages. One idea per page — if a page needs a second paragraph, it is two pages. Every page carries the index chip so a reader who lands mid-deck knows where they are. The cover has no body copy and the CTA has no data; both rules exist because the middle pages already do that work.

LinkedIn ingests carousels as PDF documents, so export the pages at 1080 × 1350 and combine them into a single PDF in page order — one image per PDF page, no bleed, no crop marks. Keep the file under 100 MB and under 300 pages (never a real constraint at ten pages). Text stays inside the 80px margin; LinkedIn's own page-turn control overlays the bottom-centre of the frame in the mobile viewer.
